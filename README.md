# AI Lead Management System

An AI-powered Lead Management System built using **n8n, OpenAI, Google Sheets, and Gmail**. This automation helps businesses handle incoming leads faster by automatically processing lead information, identifying customer requirements, organizing data, and sending personalized responses.

## 🚀 How It Works

1. A new lead submits their information through a website form or webhook.
2. The workflow receives the lead data in **n8n**.
3. The information is processed and structured automatically.
4. An **AI Agent powered by OpenAI** analyzes the lead and identifies their requirements.
5. The lead details and AI results are saved in **Google Sheets**.
6. A personalized email response is automatically sent through **Gmail**.

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- **n8n** – Workflow automation
- **OpenAI** – AI-powered lead analysis and classification
- **Google Sheets** – Lead data storage and management
- **Gmail** – Automated personalized email responses
- **Webhook** – Receiving new lead information

## 🔄 Workflow Overview

**New Lead → Webhook → Data Processing → AI Agent → Google Sheets → Automated Email**

## 💼 Business Benefits

- Reduces manual lead handling
- Provides faster responses to potential clients
- Automatically organizes lead information
- Uses AI to understand customer requirements
- Saves time and improves workflow efficiency
